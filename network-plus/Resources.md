# Network+ (N10-009) — Learning Resources

Courses and material I used to actually learn the content.

---

## Video courses

**[Professor Messer — N10-009 Training Course](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLG49S3nxzAnl_tQe3kvnmeMid0mjF8Le8)**

Free, complete, and sequenced one video per objective. The obvious starting point — the structure means your notes map cleanly onto the objectives list, and short videos make it easy to loop back on a single topic later without rewatching a whole chapter.


**[PowerCert — CompTIA Network+ N10-009 Full Certification Course](https://youtu.be/CY4hn70K3r8)**

A single full-length video covering the N10-009 material, built around animated diagrams. The visuals are the reason to watch it — topologies, encapsulation, subnetting, and routing behavior land faster when you can see the packet move than when you're reading a bulleted list.
